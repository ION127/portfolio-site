StockPulse is a solo project, so I did all of it: planning, development, infrastructure and operations. Here it is in the order I built it:

### Standing up the pipeline on the cluster

- On an on-prem Kubernetes cluster I set up ArgoCD (GitOps) and CI on a self-hosted runner, then deployed the FastAPI backend, the frontend and the Kafka pipeline.
- I encrypted secrets with Sealed Secrets so they could live in Git, and configured NFS PVs, MetalLB and Ingress (WebSocket), and Harbor certificate distribution.
- Kafka, ZooKeeper and TimescaleDB kept crashing over resource and probe problems; memory limits, TCP probes and turning off service links fixed them one by one ([incident](#incident-stockpulse-kafka-memory), [incident](#incident-stockpulse-service-links)).

### Features and observability

- I built Korean 1-minute bar ingestion (KIS) and daily-bar lookup, login, a watchlist, real-time portfolio P&L and a responsive layout.
- I added the LLM analysis report pipeline with a retry when an analysis fails. Detection runs only during market hours, and I cut down requests on weekends and holidays.
- Prometheus and Grafana (Helm), kube-state-metrics and node-exporter made the cluster and anomaly-detector metrics visible.

### Stabilizing and predicting

- I fixed ArgoCD permanently showing OutOfSync because of StatefulSet volumeClaimTemplates ([incident](#incident-stockpulse-argocd-outofsync)).
- I switched the LLM from Gemini to Groq (llama-3.3-70b) for its larger free token allowance.
- I trained a price prediction model with sector ETF features, relative strength and purged walk-forward cross-validation, showed it on the dashboard, and marked anomalies on the chart with what happened after detection (trend held or faded).
