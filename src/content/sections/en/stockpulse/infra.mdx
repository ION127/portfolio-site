### On-prem Kubernetes

- A single `stock` namespace runs 12 Deployments, 3 StatefulSets (Kafka ×3, ZooKeeper, TimescaleDB), 2 DaemonSets, 6 CronJobs and 3 Jobs.
- Traffic enters through one MetalLB (L2) address and an nginx Ingress: `/api`, `/ws`, `/auth` and `/metrics` go to FastAPI, `/grafana` and `/prometheus` to monitoring, everything else to Next.js. WebSocket timeouts are raised to one hour.
- Storage combines static NFS PVs (Retain) with nfs-subdir-external-provisioner, and heavy workloads (Kafka, the database, the API, ML) are preferentially scheduled on `node-tier=heavy` nodes.
- Images live in a private Harbor, and a DaemonSet distributes Harbor’s self-signed certificate to each node’s containerd.

### From git push to deploy

GitHub Actions on a self-hosted runner maps changed paths to services and builds **only the services that changed**. It pushes `stock/<svc>:<sha7>` to Harbor and commits the new tags to the manifests; ArgoCD polls the repo every 60 seconds, picks up that commit and syncs it to the cluster. Secrets are encrypted with Sealed Secrets before they go into Git.

### Retention and cost

- Two weekly CronJobs handle NFS usage checks (warning at 80%) and Redis AOF rewrites respectively.
- Kafka retention is capped at 7 days or 15 GiB per partition, whichever comes first.
